About FearLauncher Network

Who We Are

Welcome to the FearLauncher Network, a premium Minecraft customization and authentication platform designed specifically for passionate players. We bridge the gap between offline play and custom skin rendering, bringing you an immersive multiplayer experience where your unique style always shines.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build highly optimized, secure, and intuitive tools that enhance the way you interact with Minecraft. From custom skins and capes, to daily login coin bonuses and modular asset management (mods, resource packs, shaders, and plugins), we strive to empower every player with professional-grade capabilities completely free of charge.

Key Features

  • High-Performance Launcher Integration: Safe, seamless connection to multiplayer servers.
  • Dynamic 3D Skins & Capes: Beautiful, live rendering of classic and slim skin types.
  • Interactive Rewards & Achievements: Daily coin claims and cosmetic purchases.
  • Curated Modification Center: Verified mods, resource packs, and shaders cataloged for players.

Developed with by the FearLauncher Network Team.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At FearLauncher Network, accessible from our official application, protecting player privacy is one of our primary objectives. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected, stored, and verified by our system, and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

To register, play, and sync custom skins, we collect and store:

  • Account Details: Usernames and encrypted password hashes. We do not store plaintext passwords.
  • Game Profile Assets: Custom skin and cape PNG textures uploaded in raw Base64 strings.
  • Onboarding Demographics: Anonymous choices of country and pronoun preferences collected upon registration.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Verify Minecraft player sessions and render skin/cape textures correctly inside multiplayer games using the Yggdrasil standard API.
  • Aggregate generalized dashboard demographics in the admin dashboard.
  • Enforce fair play, prevent bans evasion, and securely sync daily coin balances.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

Our website serves clean, non-intrusive advertisements supplied by Google AdSense. Google AdSense uses cookies, such as the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements based on your visit to this and other websites on the Internet. Players may opt out of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

4. Security and Protection

We utilize industry-standard cryptographic techniques, including robust salt-hashing algorithms, to safeguard account data. No personal data is rented, sold, or shared with third parties for commercial gains.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our server administrators.
