Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

CraftynMC ("we", "us") runs the CraftynMC website and the FearLauncher client. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. By using CraftynMC you agree to this policy.

Short version: we store your account details so you can log in and play, we count anonymous visit/download numbers, and we show ads through a third-party ad network to keep the service free. We never sell your data.

1. What we collect

Account data

  • Username and display name
  • Password — stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We never see or keep your real password.
  • Your Minecraft UUID (generated offline for the launcher), skin and cape images you upload
  • Profile bio, description and avatar you choose to add
  • Coins, daily-streak, referral and reward history

Usage data

  • Approximate online status (a "last seen" timestamp) so the site can show who is online — used for the presence panel and leaderboards
  • Project view and download counts (aggregate numbers)
  • IP address and basic request metadata, used to keep the service secure and to stop abuse (rate limiting, spam and attack prevention)

Advertising data

We show advertising supplied by HilltopAds and similar ad networks. Those networks may set cookies or read your device information to choose and measure ads. We do not control those cookies — please see the network's own privacy policy.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account, log you in, and let the launcher authenticate you
  • To display your profile, skins and projects to other players
  • To run the coin economy, daily rewards, leaderboards and referrals
  • To keep the service safe: rate limiting, abuse prevention, ban enforcement
  • To understand traffic and downloads so we can improve the site
  • To serve ads, which is how CraftynMC stays free

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share only what is needed with:

  • Hosting & database providers that run the service
  • Ad networks (e.g. HilltopAds) — for serving and measuring ads
  • Image hosts — if you choose to link a Google Drive image, that image is fetched from Google
  • Law enforcement, if we are legally required to

4. Children

CraftynMC is not directed at children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. If you are under the age where you can consent in your country, please use the site only with a parent or guardian. If we learn that we hold data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

5. Data retention & your rights

Account data is kept while your account exists. Aggregate counts are kept indefinitely and contain no personal details. You may ask us to:

  • Show you the data we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete your account and its personal data

To make a request, contact us through our Discord server or the contact details on the site.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, traffic is served over HTTPS, and access to the database is limited. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above always shows the current version; continuing to use CraftynMC after a change means you accept it.

Content Rules

Last updated: 21 October 2026

These rules are part of our Terms of Use and apply to everything users upload or post — projects, descriptions, images, profiles and comments.

1. Prohibited content

Content must obey the law and must not:

  • be defamatory, obscene, abusive, hateful, harassing or violent;
  • promote sexual content, discrimination, or illegal activity;
  • infringe anyone's copyright, trademark or other rights;
  • impersonate a person or organisation, or pretend to be endorsed by us;
  • mislead — no fake claims, fake download counts or fake authors;
  • contain malware, spyware, miners or hidden remote uploads;
  • help players bypass Mojang's or a server's restrictions.

2. Clear and honest descriptions

Your project page must explain, plainly: what the project does, why someone should download it, and anything they must know before installing. Do not bury important information.

3. Cheats and hacks

A project may not be a multiplayer cheat — meaning it must not give an unfair advantage without the server being able to opt out. This includes X-ray/see-through-blocks, aim assist or aimbot, flight or speed, auto-PvP, and item duplication.

4. Copyright and reuploads

Upload only what you made or have permission to share. Re-uploading someone else's project without permission is not allowed. A "fork" (a substantially modified copy) is fine where the original licence allows it — credit the original author and link back to it.

5. AI-generated content

Be honest about AI. If a project's code, assets or design rely on generative AI, say so on the project page. Projects that are entirely or mostly AI-generated output are not allowed, and AI-generated images are not allowed as icons, gallery images or avatars.

6. Reporting

If you find something that breaks these rules, tell us — use the report option on the project or contact us through our Discord. Reports are reviewed and can lead to content removal and account bans.

Licensing

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Project licences

Every project on CraftynMC should state the licence it is released under. The licence tells other players what they are allowed to do with the download — whether they may redistribute it, modify it, include it in a modpack, or use it in a video.

Common choices you can use:

  • All Rights Reserved — others may download and play it, but may not redistribute or modify it.
  • MIT — anyone may use, modify and redistribute it, as long as they keep the copyright notice.
  • Apache-2.0 — like MIT, plus an explicit patent grant.
  • GPL-3.0 — anyone may modify and redistribute it, but their version must stay under the same licence.
  • LGPL-3.0 — like GPL, but others may link to it without re-licensing their own work.
  • CC-BY-4.0 / CC-BY-SA-4.0 — good for resource packs and art; attribution (and share-alike) required.
  • CC0 / Public Domain — no restrictions at all.
  • Custom — your own terms, as long as they are stated on the project page.

If you upload content you did not create, you must have the right to distribute it and you must follow its original licence.
