About CraftynMC

An independent Minecraft platform — built and run by players.

CraftynMC is a free home for Minecraft content and for the people who make it. We run a mod, plugin, resource pack and shader store, a 3D skin and cape customiser, a player directory, weekly leaderboard seasons, and our own Android client — FearLauncher — which lets you play Minecraft: Java Edition on your phone.

📦 A store that respects creators

Every project shows its licence, its versions, its creator and its reviews. Downloads are free.

🎮 A real launcher

FearLauncher is built for phones — tuned render resolution, a smooth FPS profile and no hidden frame cap.

🏆 Seasons

Two weekly leaderboards with real coin prizes, paid automatically. Staff never compete.

🔍 Player lookup

See any player's character in 3D with their own skin, and download their skin and cape.

How we keep it free

CraftynMC is free to use and always will be. Hosting, bandwidth and development are paid for by advertising and by the community's support. We do not sell user data, and we do not charge for downloads.

You can also earn coins on the site by watching a sponsored ad, claiming daily rewards, or inviting friends — and spend them in the Coin Shop on things like a Premium badge or an ad-free pass.

Who runs it

CraftynMC is an independent, player-run project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Mojang Synergies AB or Microsoft. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang Synergies AB.

Report a problem

  • Broken download or a project that will not install — tell us the project name and your Minecraft version.
  • Content that breaks our rules — send the link; we review every report.
  • Your account — if you are locked out, contact us from our Discord and we will help you get back in.
  • Your data — you can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account, at any time.
